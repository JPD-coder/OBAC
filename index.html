Open Book

For decades, homeowners have been asked to trust prices they couldn't verify. Open Book changes that.

Open Book is a consumer cost-intelligence platform that exposes the underlying economics of major home-service purchases. Rather than comparing what contractors charge, Open Book helps consumers understand what equipment, materials and installation actually cost.

That knowledge cuts both ways, and it is supposed to. It gives consumers what they need to recognize a fair price — and it gives good contractors the opportunity to earn an honest profit.
